# Support

## Questions and ideas

Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/yotamon/Liquid-Loom/discussions) for setup questions, architecture conversations, examples, and show-and-tell. Search existing discussions before opening a new one.

## Reproducible bugs

Open a [bug report](https://github.com/yotamon/Liquid-Loom/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with your operating system, Node and pnpm versions, the command, a minimal source tree, and complete sanitized output. Run `pnpm doctor` and include its results.

## Security

Do not use Discussions or public issues for vulnerabilities. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

Community support covers Liquid Loom’s mapping, build, CLI, starter, tests, and documented integration points. Store-specific Liquid design, app behavior, Shopify account access, and private deployment infrastructure remain the implementing team’s responsibility.
